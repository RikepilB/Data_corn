- **No personal data** (birthdays, addresses, phone numbers) in the project.
- **Uploads:** the project page accepts images only (**JPG, PNG or GIF, ≤10 MB**). No video or
  document uploads, so link to those instead.

## 4. Demo rules and judging

**Demo rules**

- Format: a video **≤30 s**, or a deck of **≤7 slides** that includes the title and intro
  slides. Over the limit means **no Global Judging**.
- Hosting: an external host (YouTube, Google Drive, GitHub, etc.) with a **public** link. It
  can't be uploaded to the Space Apps site.
- Language: English. A video in Spanish is allowed **only with English subtitles**.
- Recommended video beats: 0:00 names and team → 0:02 challenge and motivation → 0:05 title and
  tagline → 0:10 how it works and the NASA data used → 0:20–0:30 the future impact.
- A deck must show: team name, member names, the challenge, an image of the project, the user
  experience, and how NASA and space-agency data make it possible.
- The demo mainly drives the **Presentation** score; judges also evaluate the full project page.

**The five criteria (each scored 1–5)**

| Criterion | How Field Shift earns it |
|---|---|
| **Impact** | A rotation choice for corn producers on a declining aquifer (Ogallala). The real decision is which rotation holds up in a dry year and how much water it needs. The method extends to any US corn county. |
| **Creativity** | Splitting results by dry / normal / wet years instead of averaging hides fewer failures. The planting window and tasseling–silking stress sit inside a rotation comparison, not as a stand-alone planting app (2024 prior art). Automatic bare-window detection and the alien-perspective storytelling add to this. |
| **Validity** | The FAO-56 water balance is checked against SMAP anomalies, with stated limits on resolution and uncertainty (9 km pixel; scenarios, not forecasts). |
| **Relevance** | NASA data is core to every module: POWER (climate, soil temperature), SMAP, MODIS NDVI and GRACE. It answers the challenge's question about rotations, soil health and resilience directly. |
| **Presentation** | One clear demo flow: draw a plot → see the calendar → see the matrix. Use a farmer story; the official guide says to give it equal weight with the other four. |

Global award categories that fit: Best Use of Data, Best Use of Science, Local Impact, Best
Storytelling.

## 5. Legal, IP, brand and SI rules that constrain the build

- **Licence our code:** original content must be OSI open source. If none is specified, NASA
  distributes it under **Apache-2.0**. Add a `LICENSE` file (Apache-2.0 is the simplest match).
- **Third-party materials** (code and data that don't come from NASA or a partner agency) must be
  freely available or OSI-licensed. **Their licences must appear in our documentation**, or NASA
  won't post the solution. We are responsible for checking this, and for keeping out
  export-controlled (ITAR/EAR) content.
- **Cite every source**, including open-source ones. NASA can **revoke a nomination or award** for
  missing citations. Keep a `docs/REFERENCES.md` as we go.
- **Copyright:** use only music, images and text we have permission to use. That means no
  copyrighted soundtrack in the video, and royalty-free assets with the source recorded.
- **Publication and publicity:** submitting grants NASA permission to display and distribute the
  solution. Videos are licensed royalty-free to the U.S. Government, and names and likeness may
  be used. Anyone 18 or older who appears on screen must agree to this.
- **Brand:** no NASA insignia. Don't modify NASA branding with SI. The Space Apps brand page sets
  rules for the *Space Apps* logo, colours and fonts (Fira Sans, Overpass); use them only if we
  follow them. Our own visual identity must be original.
- **SI disclosure:** watermark images and video; label code and data in text and metadata;
  fill in the SI field. The idea itself must not come from SI.
- **Content bans:** no commercial promotion, no personal data, no minors, no offensive content,
  and no proprietary material we don't have the rights to.
